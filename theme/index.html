About AI Codex

Technology for Good

AI Codex is the research & development arm of OpenMind Projects — building open-source tools and curricula that harness artificial intelligence to transform education in underserved communities.

Our Mission

Democratize access to AI and coding education, empowering disadvantaged youth to become creators of technology, not just consumers.

Our Vision

A world where every young person, regardless of background, has the skills and tools to shape their future through technology.

Our Approach

We combine hands-on coding bootcamps, AI-assisted learning tools, and cross-cultural volunteer mentorship to create lasting impact.

What We Do

Three Pillars of Impact

Our programs are built on three interconnected pillars that together create a comprehensive ecosystem for digital empowerment.

Educational & Cross-Cultural Training

Volunteers help train youth and adults in IT, programming, and English. Gain cross-cultural skills while contributing to educational projects that create positive impact.

  • Cross-cultural exchange
